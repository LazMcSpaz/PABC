## What shipped this session

**Quick wins (10)** — Oswald display font, board frame brackets, "scrap" not
"resource", quieter round-1 event feed, camera centred on your capital, real
Tech Wheel node names, real unit names, formatters for 58 unformatted event
types, fixed `npm run shots`, roadmap brought current.

**Diplomacy tiers 1 and 2** — Make Peace is refusable; Denounce costs Honor
and has a cooldown; one deal-item schema with struck deals actually forming
what they promise; Demand Tribute delivers the amount set; flows carry a
duration and expire; pending offers with expiry; counter-offers that return
the nearest deal they would take; a Standing cost to over-asking.

**Warranted denouncement** — `denounceWarrant()`. Denouncing a tyrant now
*gains* Honor; denouncing without grounds costs it, and the accusation is
judged for credibility *before* the Honor hit lands (that ordering was a real
bug — a baseless denouncement used to silence itself).

**The five depth layers** — grievance ledger you can be paid to settle;
reputation gated on what the board actually witnessed (fog of war now feeds
Menace via `witnessShare`); receipts showing the player how they got here;
claims; ultimatums (`issueUltimatum` / `answerUltimatum`, with AI
follow-through and a per-pair cooldown so the AI stops bluffing itself).

**Envoy audiences** — Civ-style dialogue boxes. Offers and ultimatums used to
sit silently in the drawer; now somebody comes to say them and takes an
answer. Two portrait registers in `src/prototype/factionPortraits.js`:
`LEADER_PORTRAITS` for the irreversible moments (war, peace, pacts,
coalitions, vassalage, recognition, elimination — see `toneForEvent()`) and
`ENVOY_PORTRAITS` for everything routine. All four faction envoys are in.
`EnvoyModal.jsx` is a shared `Audience` shell with three faces — warning,
offer, demand — and temperament-keyed flavour text.
